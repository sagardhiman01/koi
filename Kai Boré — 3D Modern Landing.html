Our Story

A Gem in the
Heart of Goa

Nestled in Benaulim, South Goa, Kai Boré is where Goan soul meets global flavour. Step inside to vibrant mosaic art, warm lampshades and an energy that shifts effortlessly from lazy afternoon lunches to lively evening dinners.

From fresh-caught seafood prepared the Goan way to Korean-inspired bites and handcrafted cocktails — every plate tells a story of passion and Goa's timeless coastal spirit.
